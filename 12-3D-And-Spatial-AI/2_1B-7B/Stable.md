## 📋 Specifications
- **Category**: 3D Generation & Spatial Intelligence
- **Organization**: Stability AI
- **Parameter Count**: 1.2B
- **License**: Stability Community License

## 🎯 AOMe Implementation & Use Case
Generates UV-unwrapped 3D meshes with PBR materials (normal and roughness maps) in 0.5s.

## ⚙️ Hardware & Deployment Requirements
- **Tier**: 1B - 7B (High-Poly Textured 3D Assets)
- **Estimated Requirements**: 8GB - 16GB VRAM
- **Recommendation**: Deploy as an API service on cloud GPUs (RunPod, Lambda Labs, Modal) or stream via Ollama.
