# 3D Generation & Spatial Intelligence

## 📌 Niche Overview
- **AOMe Role**: Single-image to 3D mesh, text-to-3D, NeRF/Gaussian splatting, game asset generation, and AR/VR spatial models.
- **Organization Strategy**: Arranged strictly from low parameter counts (sub-1B) up to billions and trillions of parameters.
