# Text-to-Speech (TTS & Voice Generation)

## 📌 Niche Overview
- **AOMe Role**: Conversational voice agents, audio narration, interactive voice personas, and accessibility.
- **Organization Strategy**: Arranged strictly from low parameter counts (sub-1B) up to billions and trillions of parameters.
